📊 Data Transparency

Live numerical data: NASA POWER daily point API.

Other datasets: Resource links only; not integrated into the calculations yet.

Location: The coordinates entered above.

Data period: The date range shown after a successful API response.

Limitations: Grid-based satellite and model data may differ from conditions measured directly on a farm.
