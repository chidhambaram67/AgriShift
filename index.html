Farm Adaptation Strategies

Practical approaches for changing climate conditions.

1. Baseline Farming

Use local seasonal practices and monitor rainfall and temperature.

  • Follow local agriculture department guidance.
  • Record rainfall and irrigation.
  • Choose suitable crop varieties.

2. Soil Recovery

Improve soil health and water retention through sustainable practices.

  • Use suitable compost and organic matter.
  • Use mulch where appropriate.
  • Prevent soil erosion.

3. Climate Resilient Farming

Prepare for variable rainfall and heat using locally suitable practices.

  • Plan water storage and efficient irrigation.
  • Consider crop diversification.
  • Check local weather advisories.

NASA data information
This project uses the NASA POWER Daily API. Temperature, precipitation, humidity and solar radiation are gridded estimates for the selected coordinates, not measurements from an individual farm. Soil wetness (GWETTOP) is requested separately and may not always be available. It is a model-based wetness fraction, not a direct farm sensor reading. The 30-day charts show historical daily data, not future forecasts. Data may be delayed.
