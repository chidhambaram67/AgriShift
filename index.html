Crop Rotation Strategy Comparison

Three possible farming approaches
01 · REFERENCE APPROACH

Baseline

Continue the existing crop sequence and compare its performance against alternative strategies.

  • Maintain current crop practices
  • Monitor rainfall and irrigation needs
  • Track input use and crop performance
  • Use as the comparison reference
02 · SOIL RECOVERY

Pulse-Based Rotation

Introduce suitable pulses into the rotation to support soil health and diversify farm production.

  • Consider locally suitable pulse crops
  • Legumes can contribute nitrogen to soil
  • Use crop residues where appropriate
  • Check season, soil and water availability
03 · CLIMATE RESILIENT

Millet-Based Strategy

Consider suitable millets as part of a diversified approach for farms facing water limitations.

  • Evaluate locally suitable millet varieties
  • Review seasonal rainfall patterns
  • Consider water availability
  • Compare costs, duration and market demand

These are planning strategies, not guaranteed yield or profit predictions. Confirm crop selection with local agricultural experts and soil-test results.

Data and Method

Weather information

Temperature, humidity, rainfall and wind are retrieved from Open-Meteo's weather forecast service.

Soil moisture

Soil water content is retrieved from a weather-model dataset. It is not a laboratory soil test or a field sensor reading.

NASA climate context

NASA POWER can be added for historical climate comparisons. Historical data should not be labelled as live weather.
