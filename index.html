Farm Adaptation Strategies

Practical approaches to help farms adapt to changing climate conditions.

1. Baseline Farming

Use local seasonal practices and monitor changing rainfall and temperature.

  • Follow local agricultural department guidance.
  • Keep records of rainfall and irrigation.
  • Choose suitable crop varieties.

2. Soil Recovery

Improve soil health and help retain water through sustainable field practices.

  • Use suitable organic matter and compost.
  • Use mulch where appropriate.
  • Prevent soil erosion.

3. Climate Resilient Farming

Prepare for variable rainfall and heat using locally suitable practices.

  • Plan water storage and efficient irrigation.
  • Consider crop diversification.
  • Check local weather advisories before field operations.

NASA data information
This project uses the NASA POWER Daily API. Temperature, precipitation, relative humidity and solar radiation are gridded estimates for the selected coordinates. Soil wetness (GWETTOP) is requested separately from the Hydrology community and may not always be available. It is a model-based wetness fraction, not a direct measurement from your farm. The latest available date can be delayed. The 30-day charts show historical daily data, not future forecasts.
