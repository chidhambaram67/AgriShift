♻️ Crop Rotation Strategies

Compare three approaches to sustainable agriculture.

STRATEGY COMPARISON
STRATEGY 01

🌾 Baseline

Maintain the existing cropping system and current field management practices.

  • Continue existing crop sequence.
  • Monitor field and soil conditions.
  • Maintain current irrigation practices.
  • Record seasonal crop performance.
STRATEGY 02

🌱 Soil Recovery

Improve soil health through suitable crop rotation and organic matter management.

  • Consider suitable legume rotations.
  • Use compost and organic matter.
  • Monitor soil moisture.
  • Reduce unnecessary soil disturbance.
STRATEGY 03

🌦️ Climate Resilient

Adapt crop planning to rainfall patterns and changing environmental conditions.

  • Consider drought-tolerant varieties.
  • Plan around seasonal rainfall.
  • Improve water-use efficiency.
  • Monitor climate information.

These are general strategy examples for project comparison. Actual crop rotation decisions require local soil testing, water availability, season and agricultural expert guidance.
