    ♻️ Sustainable Farming Strategies

    Compare approaches for long-term farm resilience

    🌾 1. Baseline Farming

    Conventional crop management using existing practices.

    • Continue familiar crop choices
    • Monitor seasonal rainfall
    • Plan irrigation carefully

    🌱 2. Soil Recovery

    Improve soil health with suitable crop rotation and organic matter.

    • Include suitable pulse crops
    • Add compost where appropriate
    • Reduce soil erosion

    🌦️ 3. Climate Resilient

    Adapt farm planning to changing rainfall and temperature patterns.

    • Consider suitable millet varieties
    • Use water-saving irrigation
    • Check local weather updates
