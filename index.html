🌾 Crop Rotation Strategy Comparison

Strategy 01

Existing Farm Rotation

Record the farmer's current crop sequence, irrigation practices and observed field conditions. Use this as the baseline for comparison.

Strategy 02

Soil Improvement Rotation

Explore suitable crop sequences that may support soil cover, organic matter and soil condition. Confirm crop suitability locally.

Strategy 03

Water-Saving Rotation

Compare crop sequences using water demand, rainfall history and available irrigation information.

These are comparison frameworks, not automatic crop prescriptions. Final rotation choices should consider soil tests, local agricultural guidance, irrigation availability, season and market conditions.

ℹ️ About the NASA Data

NASA POWER provides daily solar and meteorological time-series data. This dashboard requests the selected location's data through the NASA POWER Daily Point API. Values shown are historical or near-real-time data, not a weather forecast or direct farm sensor readings.

NASA POWER data can represent a broader grid cell rather than the exact conditions of an individual field. Use local observations and soil tests when making farm decisions.
